# Arena incendiary prototype — 2026-09-30

## Player-facing contract

Opt in with F12, **Arena incendiary prototype / Enabled** (default false).
The exact local player's RGD-5 becomes an incendiary only during live Arena
combat, with the matching BigBrain companion loaded. The existing grenade
model, inventory action and throw animation remain. No new template or server
change; a bottle model/separate item is future work. Bot grenades, tripwires,
other grenade types and ordinary raids stay native.

An upward ground impact ignites up to roughly three metres of nearby level
floor for six seconds. Walls, steep surfaces, separate floors and Arena bounds
restrict the footprint. Native fuse expiry is the fallback; an airborne expiry
without a safe nearby floor fizzles. No explosion/fragments on converted throws.
Damage is 80 base total HP/s, in quarter-second steps, without stacking overlapping
fires or catching up after a hitch. Native actor damage modifiers and immunity
remain applicable. Self-damage is possible; existing TDM friendly-fire protection
and Tactical teammate exclusion are preserved. A respawned successor is not
damaged by its own previous body's fire.

Prototype particles, radial texture, light and looping crackle are generated by
our code, with no imported media or third-party assets. They are placeholders;
this is not Counter-Strike's fluid fire-spread simulation. Maximum four fires and
eight pending throws. Disabling, round breaks and leaving retire owned projectiles
and extinguish their effects. Failed projectile teardown remains suppressed and
terminally cancelled, so retry cannot ignite fire in a later round.

## Integration and ownership

- Capture the native returned grenade after `BaseGrenadeHandsController.
  CreateAndRegisterGrenade`, checking exact hands, body, weapon and bridge.
  Replace only the parameterless `Grenade.Explosion` for that registered instance.
  Native `InvokeBlowUpEvent`/`OnExplosion` still destroy/unregister the projectile.
- `ArenaFireRuntime` binds exact match, world and scoring round. No global
  template, health or navmesh edits. Floor/wall probes fail closed if saturated.
- `Flame` damage uses the frozen original thrower/weapon, distributes damage
  over living parts and normalizes EFT's per-part environment multipliers.
  A scoped exact ownership check admits those hits to existing HP/assist/kill
  capture only. Ordinary fire remains environmental. No synthetic headshots.
- Existing corpse cleanup retains that exact thrower body through pending fire
  and its short callback quiet period. It never redirects to a respawned body.
  Retention and the world reference are released after expiry or world change.
- BigBrain layer `Arena Combat Ground Fire Escape`, priority 79, samples current
  and predicted bot positions and selects a short complete route out of fire.
  Bounded searches rotate directions, preserve Arena limits and cannot reenter
  fire after leaving it. No collider or navmesh obstacle is spawned.
- Native/SAIN door actions, grenade/BTR/artillery escape and flashing retain
  precedence. Movement is handed off through the existing SAIN bridge; exact
  mover/path leases, progress checks and action-data identity prevent stale
  orders from stopping a replacement owner.

Known prototype limitation: SAIN priority-80 AvoidThreat includes DogFight, so a
close-combat action may outrank this layer. Supported brains are the companion's
PMC/Assault/ExUsec/Obdolbs/CursAssault set, not every boss brain. Native avoidance,
visual quality, sound mix and performance require a raid; desktop checks do not
certify them.

## Evidence

Native seams checked in the installed decompilation: `Grenade.cs` collision,
timer, explosion and destruction; `Player.cs` grenade creation and attributed
damage; `ActiveHealthController.cs` environment multipliers; native mover and
BigBrain action switching; installed SAIN door/avoid-threat behavior.

`tools/MolotovChecks/Verify.ps1`: 57 assertions against linked production runtime,
geometry, policy and bot layer, plus eight compiled negative controls. Includes
ordinary-raid isolation, exact throw ownership, posthumous attribution, overlap,
pause/hitch behavior, floor/wall bounds, teardown retry, lifecycle release, door
ownership, bounded route selection and lease rollover. Unity/native boundaries
are doubles, not real physics/AI acceptance.

Related checks: AssistCapture 130 assertions, five negative controls and 39
native IL contracts; Assist policy 431; corpse cleanup 213; DynamicMaps corpse
61; announcer combat 194; TeamGiveUp verification passed. Staged client and
companion build without changing installed client/server/core/companion files.

Independent adversarial review found and corrected damage overlap, native
environment normalization, old-body assists, false headshots, corpse/world
retention, stale bot leases, search starvation and cancelled teardown retries.
Native delivery/acceptance is recorded separately after the final build; no GPU
pass is inferred from these tests.

## Manual acceptance

1. Restart EFT with client and companion from the same build. In an Arena
   Deathmatch, enable the single prototype setting and throw a kit RGD-5 onto
   open ground: no blast, visible/audible six-second fire, then complete cleanup.
2. Stand in the fire and move out; check predictable health loss and cessation.
   Throw near an enemy: escape movement, attributed kill/assist, no headshot badge.
3. Throw beside a wall/on a platform; no damage through walls or to another floor.
   Check door ownership and repeated throws; no lingering movement after expiry.
4. Throw, die/respawn, then disable during another pending throw; no reassignment,
   native explosion fallback, persistent particles or late next-round ignition.
5. Setting off and ordinary PMC control: RGD-5 retains its normal explosion.
   TDM friendly protection and Tactical round transitions need separate checks.
