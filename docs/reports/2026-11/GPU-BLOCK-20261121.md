## The finding that changed the build

**The first Team DM start was refused with USEC 0/10 safe connected places.** The formation needs, for each of the ten
USEC points, a floor, a clear capsule and a NavMesh connection to the donor, and it needs the anchor to pass the same
tests before any held path counts. All ten south points failed, so the capacity was 0 of 10 against 4 required.

The cause, as the commit and the survey state it: Arena authors the six `Respawn_Flat` platform markers 0.101 to 0.103
m under their collision floor (7.2673 and 7.2653 against 7.368), and `Usec house (8)` 0.214 m under its floor (7.2693
against 7.483). The runtime's `FloorAndClear` (`ArenaTeamBoxRuntime.cs`) raycasts from 0.25 m above the point and
accepts a hit within 0.15 m of the point, and then tests a clearance capsule whose lowest sphere reaches 0.10 m above
the point (end-sphere centres 0.45 m and 1.45 m above it, radius 0.35 m). A floor 0.101 m above the point is inside that
capsule, so the six markers failed the capsule, and `(8)` failed the 0.15 m ray tolerance. The USEC anchor `south-05`
is one of the six, so no held path was safe. The first admission commit had seen only the 0.15 m ray rule and
substituted `(8)` alone. The log shows the count 0/10; no capsule query was logged, so the capsule is the cause named in
the commit, not a line of the log.

**The fix (`fb2b3af6`).** Seven south points carry the measured collision floors: 7.368 for `south-01`, `-02`, `-05`,
`-06`, `-07` and `-08`, 7.483 for `south-04`. X, Z and yaw stay the markers'; `FloorAndClear` is unchanged. The three
other south points stand on or above their floors as authored (`south-03` +0.017 m, `south-09` and `-10` 0.000 m) and
the three measured north points stand 0.043 to 0.053 m above theirs (floor 6.134; the other seven were not cast). Build 2 then
passed the same scenario with `USEC 10/10` and `BEAR 10/10`. The offline suites could not have caught this: the xunit
tests and the harnesses do not run `FloorAndClear`, which needs the game's physics.
